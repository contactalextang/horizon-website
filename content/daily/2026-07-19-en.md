## [Regressive JPEGs enable reverse-order image loading.](https://maurycyz.com/projects/bad_jpeg/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On July 17, 2026, programmer Maurycy showed how to craft JPEG files whose scans are ordered from high to low frequency, causing the image to appear in reverse as it downloads. This trick highlights a novel way to manipulate progressive JPEG encoding for visual effects, steganography, or timing-based demonstrations, inspiring creative uses despite limited practical utility. The technique rewrites the JPEG scan script to reverse the usual low‑to‑high frequency order, relies on the image being served in chunks, and its playback timing is dictated by network delay unless the server adds artificial pauses.

hackernews · Hacker News RSS · Jul 18, 03:14 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48954851)

**Background**: Standard JPEG images store all data in a single scan, while progressive JPEGs split the encoded coefficients into multiple scans ordered from low to high frequency, allowing a blurry preview to improve as more scans arrive. By reordering these scans so that high‑frequency details come first, a partially downloaded image shows sharp edges before the overall shape becomes visible. This manipulation requires customizing the scan script during encoding, a feature supported by most JPEG libraries.
