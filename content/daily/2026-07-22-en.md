## [Terry Tao examines alleged AI-generated counterexample to Jacobian conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/07/21/a-digestion-of-the-jacobian-conjecture-counterexample/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On July 21, 2026, Terry Tao published a blog post analyzing a claimed counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture that was allegedly produced by the AI model Claude Fable 5, and he included the prompts used in the AI conversation. The Jacobian conjecture is a long‑standing open problem in algebraic geometry; a verified counterexample would settle it and demonstrate AI’s growing capacity to contribute original mathematical insight. The claimed counterexample is a three‑dimensional polynomial map whose Jacobian determinant is a non‑zero constant, yet it allegedly lacks a polynomial inverse. Tao explains the example, reproduces the Claude Fable 5 prompts, and mentions that a Mathematica check was reported but the result has not been independently confirmed.

hackernews · Hacker News RSS · Jul 21, 21:09 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48998362)

**Background**: The Jacobian conjecture asserts that if a polynomial map F: ℂⁿ → ℂⁿ has a Jacobian determinant that is a non‑zero constant, then F possesses a polynomial inverse. First proposed for two variables in 1884 and formulated in full generality by Ott‑Heinrich Keller in 1939, it remains one of the most famous open problems in algebraic geometry. The conjecture is equivalent to stating that such polynomial maps are automorphisms of the polynomial ring ℂ[x₁,…,xₙ], and its resolution would have deep implications for the structure of polynomial mappings.
