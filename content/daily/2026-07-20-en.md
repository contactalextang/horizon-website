## [Last MPEG-4 Visual Patent Expires, Codec Becomes Royalty-Free](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Last-MPEG-4-Patent-Expired) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The final patent covering MPEG-4 Visual (Part 2) has expired, making the codec fully royalty-free worldwide. This removal of licensing fees lowers barriers for open-source video tools and archival projects, although its practical impact is limited compared to newer standards like H.264 and AV1. MPEG-4 Part 2, also known as MPEG-4 Visual, underlies codecs such as Xvid and DivX; the last active patent was held in Brazil, while US and EU patents had already expired.

hackernews · Hacker News RSS · Jul 19, 16:45 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48969635)

**Background**: MPEG-4 Part 2 is an older video compression standard within the MPEG-4 family, originally designed for low-bitrate video and widely used in early internet video, CD/DVD, and portable devices. It is distinct from MPEG-4 Part 10 (H.264/AVC), which provides higher efficiency and became the dominant codec for streaming and Blu-ray. The standard was finalized in 1999 and saw widespread adoption in the early 2000s before being superseded by more advanced codecs.
