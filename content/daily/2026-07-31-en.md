## [Strategies for Scaling PostgreSQL Job Queues and Mitigating MVCC Bloat](https://www.dbos.dev/blog/making-postgres-queues-scale) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The DBOS blog post outlines practical strategies for scaling PostgreSQL‑based job queues, focusing on mitigating MVCC bloat and tuning autovacuum for high‑write workloads. As more applications rely on PostgreSQL for job processing, understanding how to avoid bloat and keep autovacuum efficient is essential for maintaining performance without adding external message brokers. Techniques include using SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED for lock‑free consumer workers, adjusting autovacuum_naptime and autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit, partitioning or archiving old rows, and monitoring pg_stat_user_tables for dead tuple growth.

hackernews · Hacker News RSS · Jul 30, 18:39 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49113913)

**Background**: PostgreSQL’s MVCC creates a new tuple version on each UPDATE or DELETE, leaving the old version as a dead tuple that accumulates unless removed by VACUUM. Autovacuum is a background process that periodically cleans these dead tuples, but its default settings may be insufficient for high‑write workloads such as job queues. The FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED clause allows multiple workers to read and lock unlocked rows concurrently, forming a simple yet efficient queue inside the database.
