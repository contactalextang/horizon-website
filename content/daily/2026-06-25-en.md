## [NVIDIA's 45°C Liquid Cooling Cuts Data Center Water Use to Near Zero](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/liquid-cooling-ai-factories/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NVIDIA announced a liquid‑cooling architecture that runs coolant at 45 °C, allowing AI data centers to achieve near‑zero water consumption for cooling while maintaining chip performance. Reducing water use addresses sustainability pressures on hyperscale facilities and can save operators millions of dollars annually in water and electricity costs, making AI infrastructure greener and more economical. The design uses warm water as the heat‑transfer medium, eliminating evaporative loss; engineering estimates indicate a 50‑MW hyperscale center could save over 2.6 million gallons of water per year and more than $4 million in combined water and electricity expenses.

hackernews · Hacker News RSS · Jun 24, 14:10 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48660178)

**Background**: Traditional data center cooling relies on evaporative chillers that consume large volumes of water, especially in hot climates. Warm water cooling raises the coolant temperature to around 45 °C, allowing heat to be rejected without evaporation and enabling reuse of the water in closed‑loop systems. Examples such as NASA Ames’ modular supercomputing facility show that high‑inlet water temperatures can be efficient, and the approach can be coupled with district heating to provide useful warmth to nearby communities.
